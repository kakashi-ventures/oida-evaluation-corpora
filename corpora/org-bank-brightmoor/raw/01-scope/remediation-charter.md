# AML/BSA Remediation Program Charter — "Project Cornerstone"

**Date:** January 26, 2026
**Program Sponsor:** Marcus Hale — Chief Risk Officer
**Program Lead / BSA Officer:** Dana Whitfield — Chief Compliance Officer & designated BSA Officer
**Institution:** Brightmoor Bank, N.A. (≈$14bn-asset regional commercial bank)
**Classification:** Internal — Board Risk Committee distribution

---

## 1. Background & trigger

On January 15, 2026, the Bank received a formal examination finding — a Matter Requiring Attention (MRA) — from its primary federal regulator following the December 2025 BSA/AML examination fieldwork. The MRA cited three deficiency areas: (1) gaps in transaction-monitoring coverage, (2) a backlog of uninvestigated monitoring alerts, and (3) weaknesses in BSA/AML program governance and senior accountability.

This charter establishes the remediation program ("Project Cornerstone") to respond to the MRA and to bring the Bank's BSA/AML program back into a satisfactory posture ahead of the regulator's validation review.

## 2. Objectives

1. **Transaction monitoring.** Remediate the monitoring coverage gaps identified in the MRA, including a risk-based review of monitoring thresholds and rule coverage.
2. **Alert backlog.** Investigate and disposition the backlog of uninvestigated alerts to current standard, and stand up sustainable throughput.
3. **Governance.** Strengthen BSA/AML program governance and clarify senior accountability for the program.

## 3. Scope

- **In scope:** The Bank's automated transaction-monitoring system (Sentinel Analytics platform), the alert investigation queue, SAR decisioning, monitoring thresholds and rules, and BSA/AML program governance.
- **Dependency in scope for coordination:** The concurrent core-banking platform migration (legacy "Horizon" core → new "Unifi" core), on which the transaction-monitoring data feed depends. Project Cornerstone does not own the migration, but its monitoring outcomes depend on it.
- **Out of scope:** OFAC/sanctions screening (separately satisfactory at exam), and the broader core-migration program objectives beyond the monitoring data feed.

## 4. Workstreams & owners

1. **Transaction monitoring & thresholds** — Priya Nair (Head of Financial Crimes / AML).
2. **Alert backlog remediation** — Priya Nair (Head of Financial Crimes / AML), with contract investigators.
3. **Governance & regulatory response** — Dana Whitfield (CCO / BSA Officer).
4. **Data & core-migration coordination** — Greg Olsen (CIO), in coordination with Nair's team.

## 5. Governance of the program (as chartered)

For the duration of the remediation, **the AML team under Priya Nair (Head of Financial Crimes) owns and runs Project Cornerstone day to day**, reporting to the CCO and through her to the Board Risk Committee. The Head of Financial Crimes is accountable for program delivery; the CRO is the executive sponsor. (Note: this day-to-day ownership model was revised in March 2026 — see the revised governance memo establishing a dedicated remediation PMO under the CCO; the revision was made to strengthen senior accountability as the MRA expects.)

## 6. Target timeline

MRA response acknowledgment to the regulator within statutory window; threshold and monitoring decisions in Q1; backlog materially reduced by mid-year; remediation status report to the regulator by end of April; regulator validation thereafter.
