# Brightmoor Bank — Enterprise Risk Appetite Statement (Compliance & Financial-Crime Risk)

**Date:** January 1, 2025
**Document type:** Board-approved governing statement (annual)
**Status:** Current — durable governance reference, reviewed annually by the Board Risk Committee
**Owner:** Chief Risk Officer

---

## Purpose

This statement defines the level and types of risk Brightmoor Bank is willing to accept in pursuit of its strategy. It is a standing, board-approved reference; it does not change with individual projects or examinations. The compliance and financial-crime sections below govern all BSA/AML activity.

## Compliance & regulatory risk

The Bank maintains a **low appetite** for compliance and regulatory risk. The Bank does not accept any appetite for willful or systemic non-compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

## Financial-crime / BSA-AML risk

The Bank maintains a **zero-tolerance posture toward facilitating financial crime**, money laundering, or terrorist financing. Specifically:

- The Bank will maintain a BSA/AML program reasonably designed to detect and report suspicious activity in compliance with the Bank Secrecy Act and implementing regulations.
- The Bank accepts that effective monitoring will generate false positives; operational cost of monitoring is **not** an acceptable justification for reducing monitoring coverage below the level required to detect suspicious activity.
- The Bank's risk appetite **prioritizes detection of suspicious activity over customer-experience friction** where the two are in tension. Customer-friction concerns are to be managed through process and tuning, not through suppressing coverage.
- Uninvestigated monitoring alerts beyond defined service levels are outside appetite.

## Operational & reputational risk

The Bank maintains a moderate appetite for operational risk inherent in serving its markets, but a low appetite for operational failures that impair regulatory compliance or customer protection. Reputational harm arising from a financial-crime or compliance failure is outside appetite.

## Authority

Breaches of this appetite are escalated to the Board Risk Committee. This statement is reviewed and re-approved annually; the current version is effective for calendar year 2025 and remains the governing reference until re-approved.
