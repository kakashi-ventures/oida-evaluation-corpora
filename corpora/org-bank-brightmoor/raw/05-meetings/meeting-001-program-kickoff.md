# Meeting 001: Project Cornerstone — Program Kickoff

**Date:** January 28, 2026
**Location:** Risk Conference Room / video
**Attendees:** Dana Whitfield (CCO, chair), Marcus Hale (CRO), Priya Nair (Head of Financial Crimes), Greg Olsen (CIO), Lena Cho (Head of Retail Banking), Raj Patel (Internal Audit)
**Purpose:** Stand up the remediation program; align on the MRA and workstreams

---

[09:00] Whitfield: This is the kickoff for Project Cornerstone, our response to the January 15 MRA. Marcus is sponsor, I lead as BSA Officer, Priya's team runs the AML workstreams day to day for now. Three findings, three workstreams: monitoring and thresholds, the alert backlog, and governance.

[09:06] Hale: I want to set the tone. This is an MRA, not a consent order, and the difference is ours to keep. If we remediate credibly, it closes at validation. If we're sloppy or we spin, it escalates. So: real numbers, no spin, and we tell the regulator the truth even when it's awkward.

[09:11] Nair: On numbers — I'll have a defensible backlog figure this week. Fair warning, it'll be higher than the dashboard number, because I'm going to count auto-closed alerts as uninvestigated. The system closing an alert isn't a person reviewing it, and that's exactly what the MRA is about.

[09:15] Olsen: And it lands right on my migration. Finding 1's coverage gap is the cutover. I'll be honest that the January cutover stabilization wasn't clean from a monitoring-feed standpoint, even though the core ledger reconciled. Priya and I need to get aligned on how we describe that.

[09:19] Cho: I'll be the voice in the room you don't always want to hear. Whatever we do on thresholds has a customer-experience cost. I'm not arguing against compliance — I'm asking that we count the friction honestly when we set the threshold, not pretend it's free.

[09:23] Whitfield: That's exactly the debate I want to have in the open, Lena. We'll have a dedicated threshold session. The appetite statement gives us the tiebreaker — coverage over cost — but the *how* is where your input matters.

[09:27] Patel: Audit will shadow this. One early flag: be careful about the Sentinel vendor's 40% false-positive number. Model Risk is validating it. Don't let it into business cases until it's validated.

[09:31] Whitfield: Noted. Actions: Priya, backlog number and the data-gap picture; Greg, migration coordination; me, the formal MRA response and regulator interaction. Mondays going forward.
