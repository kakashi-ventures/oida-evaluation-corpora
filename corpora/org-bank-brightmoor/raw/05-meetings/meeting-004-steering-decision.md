# Meeting 004: Project Cornerstone Steering Committee — Binding Decisions

**Date:** March 20, 2026
**Location:** Boardroom / video
**Attendees:** Marcus Hale (CRO, chair), Dana Whitfield (CCO/BSA Officer), Priya Nair (Head of Financial Crimes), Greg Olsen (CIO), Lena Cho (Head of Retail Banking), Raj Patel (Internal Audit)
**Purpose:** Make the binding remediation decisions on threshold, migration, and governance

---

[15:00] Hale: This is the steering committee. Three decisions to make binding today: the monitoring threshold, the migration approach, and program governance. Each has been debated in its own session; we decide here. Dana keeps the record.

## Decision 1 — Monitoring threshold

[15:04] Hale: The threshold debate landed on risk-based at a $5k baseline, with Lena's friction-management conditions. Priya, anything new?

[15:05] Nair: No — recommendation stands: replace the $10,000 fixed threshold with a **$5,000 risk-based threshold**, baseline trigger flexed by customer risk and product. Friction-management plan attached per Lena.

[15:07] Cho: My conditions are in the record and Priya's accepted them. With those, I support it.

[15:08] Hale: **Decision: approved. The monitoring threshold moves to $5,000 risk-based, superseding the $10,000 fixed policy. A versioned threshold policy memo (v2) to issue.** Friction-management plan is a condition of rollout.

## Decision 2 — Migration approach

[15:12] Olsen: Risk review recommended phased over big-bang. Recommendation: adopt the **phased cutover**, validate the monitoring feed after each phase, keep the legacy fallback per phase.

[15:14] Hale: **Decision: approved. The core migration moves to a phased cutover, superseding the big-bang plan. The phased migration plan to issue.**

## Decision 3 — Program governance

[15:18] Hale: Finding 3 is about senior accountability. I've proposed moving program ownership from the AML line to a dedicated remediation PMO under the CCO. Priya keeps the fincrime workstreams; program ownership and accountability move to the PMO.

[15:20] Nair: I support it. It's the right accountability line for an MRA and it lets my team focus on the monitoring and backlog work.

[15:21] Whitfield: I'll own the PMO as CCO and BSA Officer, reporting to the Board Risk Committee.

[15:22] Hale: **Decision: approved. Program ownership moves to a dedicated remediation PMO under the CCO, superseding the day-to-day ownership model in the January charter. A revised governance memo to issue.**

[15:25] Hale: All three binding as of today. Dana, get the threshold v2 memo, the phased migration plan, and the governance memo issued this week, and fold all three into the remediation status report to the regulator. Patel, audit confirms the decisions are recorded. Adjourned.
