Dana, Priya,

I'm making a governance change to Project Cornerstone, effective on the revised governance memo Dana will issue.

We are **moving ownership of the remediation program from the AML team (Priya) to a dedicated remediation PMO that sits under the CCO (Dana).** To be clear about intent: this is not a reflection on Priya or her team — the AML work is solid, and Priya continues to own the financial-crimes workstreams (monitoring, thresholds, backlog) on the ground. What changes is *program* ownership and senior accountability.

Why: the MRA's Finding 3 is specifically about senior accountability and governance commensurate with the program's risk. A remediation owned and run out of the line function it is remediating does not, on its face, demonstrate the independent senior oversight the regulator expects. Putting the program under a dedicated PMO reporting to the CCO — who is the accountable BSA Officer and reports to the Board Risk Committee — is a cleaner accountability line and directly answers Finding 3.

This **supersedes the day-to-day program-ownership model in the January charter.** Dana will issue the revised governance memo; steering will confirm. Priya, thank you — nothing about your delivery prompted this; it's about the accountability structure the MRA wants to see.

Marcus
