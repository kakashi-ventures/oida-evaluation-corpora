Dana, Marcus, Priya,

Audit flag on the Sentinel business case. The vendor is selling a **40% false-positive reduction**, and I've seen that 40% number start to appear in our internal staffing assumptions. I want to put a marker down before it gets baked in.

Model Risk's independent validation (Feb 10) replayed our own twelve months of data and found roughly **15%**, and even that wasn't statistically robust — they say they can't reliably distinguish it from no effect on our portfolio. So we have a vendor marketing number (40%) and an independent, on-our-data number (~15%, not robust), and they don't agree. From an audit standpoint the validated figure governs, full stop — we don't get to plan around a vendor's portfolio-general claim when our own validation contradicts it.

Concrete asks: (1) strip the 40% assumption out of any staffing or capacity business case; (2) if anyone cites a false-positive reduction to the regulator or the board, it must be the validated ~15% with the "not statistically robust" caveat, not the vendor figure; (3) require Brightmoor-specific tuning and ongoing performance monitoring as a condition of use, per Model Risk's recommendation.

Happy to bring this to steering.

Raj
