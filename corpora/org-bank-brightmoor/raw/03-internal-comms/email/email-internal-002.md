# Email: Threshold change — moving to $5,000 risk-based monitoring

**From:** Priya Nair (Head of Financial Crimes)

**Cc:** Project Cornerstone distribution

**Subject:** Threshold change — moving to $5,000 risk-based monitoring

---

Team,

Following the regulator's March 2 follow-up — which was explicit that a flat **$10,000 fixed** monitoring threshold is inadequate and that they expect a **risk-based** approach — we are changing the monitoring threshold on the principal cash/structuring scenarios.

The change: **from a $10,000 fixed threshold to a $5,000 risk-based threshold.** "Risk-based" means $5,000 is the baseline trigger, but the effective threshold flexes by customer risk rating and product — lower scrutiny floors for low-risk consumer accounts, tighter ones for higher-risk segments and cash-intensive businesses. This is the model the regulator expects and the one our own risk-appetite statement already implies (coverage over cost).

I know this raises false-positive volume, and I know Retail has concerns about customer friction — Lena, let's take that head-on at the threshold meeting. But the regulator's position doesn't leave us a flat-$10k option, and our risk appetite is clear that operational cost is not a basis to cut coverage. This is going to the threshold debate Friday and then to steering for the binding call; I'm announcing the direction now so the team can start the tuning work.

Formal policy will follow as a versioned threshold memo (this supersedes the current $10k policy).

Priya
