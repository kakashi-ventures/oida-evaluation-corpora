# Remediation Status Report to the Regulator — MRA (BSA/AML)

**Date:** April 30, 2026
**Document type:** Remediation status report (Project Cornerstone PMO → Primary Federal Regulator)
**Prepared by:** Dana Whitfield, CCO / BSA Officer, on behalf of the remediation PMO
**Re:** Matter Requiring Attention transmitted January 15, 2026
**Status:** CURRENT — the Bank's authoritative statement of remediation status as submitted to the regulator

---

## 1. Purpose

This report sets out the Bank's remediation status against the three findings of the January 15, 2026 MRA. It is the current, authoritative status of the remediation as submitted to the regulator. Validation by the regulator is pending.

## 2. Status by finding

### Finding 1 — Transaction-monitoring coverage

- **Thresholds:** The Bank has replaced the prior flat **$10,000 fixed** monitoring threshold with a **$5,000 risk-based** threshold (Threshold Policy v2, effective per the March 20 steering decision), consistent with the regulator's March 2 direction. Thresholds are now documented and calibrated to customer risk and product.
- **Migration-period monitoring gap:** The Bank has identified and disclosed a period of approximately **three weeks** during the January core cutover in which the transaction-monitoring feed did not receive complete data and alerts were not generated on all covered activity. The affected transaction population is being quantified and **transaction monitoring is being re-run over the window** (look-back). To prevent recurrence, the Bank changed the remaining core migration from a big-bang cutover to a **phased cutover** with monitoring-feed validation after each phase.

### Finding 2 — Alert backlog

- The Bank's backlog, measured on an inclusive basis (counting system auto-closed alerts as uninvestigated), stood at approximately **1,400 alerts** at the start of remediation. It has since been materially reduced through added investigator capacity and risk-tiered work-down, with SAR decisioning prioritized on the high-risk queue. Sustainable throughput controls are in place. (The Bank notes for the record that an early preliminary triage characterization that "all ~1,400 alerts were SAR-eligible" was overstated and was corrected; SAR-eligibility is determined on investigation.)

### Finding 3 — Program governance and senior accountability

- The Bank has established a **dedicated remediation PMO under the Chief Compliance Officer**, superseding the prior model in which the AML line owned the program day to day. This strengthens senior accountability and board-level management information, as the finding requires.

## 3. A note on measurement candor

The Bank flags that its inclusive backlog figure (~1,400) is higher than the monitoring platform's "open alerts" dashboard figure (~900) because the Bank counts auto-closed alerts as uninvestigated; and that an independent model validation found the candidate monitoring model delivered only ~15% (not the vendor-advertised 40%) false-positive reduction on Bank data, and not statistically robustly, so the Bank has not relied on the vendor figure. These are disclosed in the interest of accuracy.

## 4. Conclusion

The Bank has completed the threshold change, the governance change, and the migration-approach change, has materially reduced the backlog, and is quantifying and re-running monitoring over the migration-gap window. The Bank requests validation and remains available to the examination team.
