# Revised Remediation Governance — Dedicated PMO Under the CCO

**Date:** March 19, 2026
**Document type:** Governance memo — Project Cornerstone
**Author:** Dana Whitfield, CCO / BSA Officer
**Endorsed:** Marcus Hale, CRO (March 18); confirmed by the Steering Committee, March 20
**Status header (as printed):** ACTIVE — **supersedes the program-ownership (governance) section of the Remediation Charter (January 26, 2026)**

---

## 1. Purpose

Revise the governance of Project Cornerstone to establish clearer senior accountability, directly addressing MRA Finding 3 (program governance and senior accountability). This memo changes how the remediation program is owned and overseen; it **supersedes the day-to-day program-ownership model set out in §5 of the January 26 Remediation Charter.** The rest of the charter (objectives, scope, workstreams) remains in force.

## 2. What changes

**Program ownership moves from the AML team to a dedicated remediation Program Management Office (PMO) under the Chief Compliance Officer.**

- Previously (per the charter): the AML team under the Head of Financial Crimes (Priya Nair) owned and ran the remediation program day to day.
- Now (per this memo): a **dedicated remediation PMO, reporting to the CCO** (Dana Whitfield, also the accountable BSA Officer), owns the program — its plan, milestones, reporting, and regulator-facing accountability.

## 3. What does not change

The Head of Financial Crimes continues to own the **financial-crimes workstreams** — transaction monitoring, thresholds, and alert-backlog remediation — on the ground. This memo changes *program* ownership and the senior-accountability line, not the delivery ownership of the technical AML work.

## 4. Why

A remediation owned and run out of the very line function being remediated does not demonstrate the independent senior oversight the MRA expects. A dedicated PMO reporting to the CCO — who is the accountable BSA Officer reporting to the Board Risk Committee — creates a clean accountability line and strengthens board-level management information, which is what Finding 3 requires.

## 5. Reporting

The PMO reports program status to the CCO and, through the CCO, to the Board Risk Committee on the agreed cadence, and owns the remediation status reporting to the regulator.

## 6. Supersession

This memo **supersedes §5 (program governance / day-to-day ownership) of the Remediation Charter dated January 26, 2026.** Where this memo and the charter's original ownership language conflict, this memo governs.
