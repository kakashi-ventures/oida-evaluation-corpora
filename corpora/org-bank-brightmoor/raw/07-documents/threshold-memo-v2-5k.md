# Transaction-Monitoring Threshold Policy v2 — $5,000 Risk-Based

**Date:** March 22, 2026
**Document type:** Monitoring threshold policy — controlled document TM-THR-001, version 2
**Author:** Priya Nair, Head of Financial Crimes
**Approved:** Project Cornerstone Steering Committee, March 20, 2026
**Status header (as printed):** ACTIVE — **supersedes Threshold Policy v1 ($10,000 fixed, February 2026)**

---

## 1. Purpose

Establish the Bank's remediated, risk-based transaction-monitoring threshold, replacing the prior $10,000 fixed threshold cited under MRA Finding 1. This policy reflects the regulator's March 2 direction that monitoring be risk-based, and the steering committee's binding decision of March 20, 2026.

## 2. Threshold (v2 — in force)

The principal cash/structuring scenario now uses a **$5,000 risk-based threshold**. Specifically:

- **$5,000 is the baseline trigger.** It replaces the flat $10,000 fixed threshold.
- The **effective threshold flexes by customer risk rating and product**: low-risk consumer accounts carry a higher effective floor to limit false positives on ordinary activity; higher-risk segments and cash-intensive business customers carry the $5,000 baseline or tighter.
- Thresholds are documented and reviewed against the customer risk profile, satisfying the "risk-based and documented" expectation.

## 3. Rationale

A flat $10,000 threshold is not risk-based and is vulnerable to structuring just below it (the regulator noted observed sub-threshold structuring). A $5,000 risk-based baseline extends coverage to the activity the prior threshold missed, while the risk-weighting keeps low-risk consumer activity from generating excessive false positives.

## 4. Customer-friction management (condition of rollout)

Per the steering decision, this policy is paired with a friction-management plan (enhanced tuning, expedited customer outreach on holds, and clear customer communications) to manage the customer-experience impact of the lower baseline. Coverage remains the governing priority where coverage and friction conflict, consistent with the Bank's risk-appetite statement.

## 5. Supersession

This v2 policy **supersedes Threshold Policy v1 ($10,000 fixed)** in its entirety. The $10,000 fixed threshold is no longer in force as of this policy.
