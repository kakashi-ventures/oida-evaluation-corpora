> **Status note:** This v1 policy documented the monitoring threshold as it stood early in the remediation, before the regulator's March 2 follow-up and the steering decision of March 20. It was **superseded by Threshold Policy v2 (March 22, 2026)**, which adopts a $5,000 risk-based threshold. v1 is retained only to show the prior state and the change. Do not apply the v1 threshold.

## 1. Scope

Monitoring thresholds for the Bank's principal cash and structuring transaction-monitoring scenarios on the Sentinel platform.

## 2. Threshold (v1 — as it stood)

The principal cash/structuring scenario uses a **$10,000 fixed monetary threshold**. A single fixed threshold is applied uniformly across customer segments. Activity at or above $10,000 in the covered scenarios generates a monitoring alert.

## 3. Known limitation (flagged at drafting)

This fixed-threshold approach is the configuration the examination criticized under Finding 1: it is not demonstrably risk-based, it was set without documented justification, and a round-number fixed threshold is vulnerable to structuring just beneath it. This v1 memo records the prior state for the file; the remediated, risk-based policy is in v2.

## 4. Disposition

Superseded by Threshold Policy v2 (effective on the steering decision of March 20, 2026; issued March 22, 2026), which replaces the $10,000 fixed threshold with a $5,000 risk-based threshold.
