## Original memo (as written February 3 — superseded)

We have pulled an initial picture of the uninvestigated alert backlog ahead of the formal report. The backlog is on the order of **~1,400 alerts**. On a fast first look, **all ~1,400 alerts appear to be SAR-eligible** and should be treated as potential SAR candidates pending investigation. We recommend escalating immediately on the assumption that the entire population may require SAR decisioning.

(Drafter's caveat at the time: this is a first-pass read done under time pressure; it has not been risk-tiered. Treat as provisional.)

*Correction applied February 6: the "all ~1,400 SAR-eligible" characterization is withdrawn. See the Alert Backlog Report (February 6, 2026) for the authoritative, risk-tiered figures, under which the great majority of the backlog is low-risk and SAR candidates are expected to concentrate in the high-risk priority queue (≈180).*
