# Matter Requiring Attention (MRA) — BSA/AML Program Deficiencies

**Date:** January 15, 2026
**Document type:** Examination finding (regulator-issued; reproduced in the Bank's MRA register)
**Examination:** Brightmoor Bank, N.A. — BSA/AML examination, fieldwork December 2025
**Status:** OPEN — remediation in progress; authoritative statement of the deficiencies

---

> This is the Bank's controlled copy of the supervisory finding communicated by the primary federal regulator on January 15, 2026. It is the authoritative description of the deficiencies the remediation program must address. Findings are reproduced below; the cover letter is filed separately.

## Finding 1 — Transaction-monitoring coverage gaps

The Bank's automated transaction-monitoring system did not provide adequate coverage during the examination period. Specifically:

- Monitoring scenario coverage had not been reviewed against the Bank's current risk profile; the fixed monetary thresholds in use (notably a **$10,000 fixed threshold** on the principal cash-structuring scenario) were not demonstrably risk-based and were set without documented justification.
- During the examination period the Bank executed a core-banking platform migration. Examiners identified a **period during the migration cutover in which transaction data did not flow completely to the monitoring system, such that alerts were not generated on all covered activity**. The Bank had not, at the time of fieldwork, fully quantified or remediated the resulting monitoring gap.

## Finding 2 — Backlog of uninvestigated alerts

The Bank had accumulated a significant backlog of transaction-monitoring alerts that had not been investigated and dispositioned within the Bank's stated service levels. Alert investigation throughput had not kept pace with alert generation, and the Bank could not demonstrate that potentially suspicious activity within the backlog had been timely reviewed for SAR filing.

## Finding 3 — Program governance and senior accountability

BSA/AML program governance required strengthening. Specifically, the lines of senior accountability for the program were not sufficiently clear, management information to the Board Risk Committee was not adequate to support oversight, and the Bank had not established a governance structure commensurate with the program's risk.

## Corrective-action expectations

The Bank is expected to: (a) submit a remediation plan; (b) perform a risk-based review of monitoring thresholds and scenario coverage; (c) remediate and quantify the migration-period monitoring gap; (d) eliminate the alert backlog and demonstrate sustainable throughput; and (e) strengthen program governance and senior accountability. The regulator will validate remediation. Progress is to be reported on the agreed cadence.
