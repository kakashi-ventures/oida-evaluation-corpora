Ms. Whitfield,

Thank you for the materials provided in support of the transaction-monitoring workstream. We are writing to give you clear direction on one point ahead of your threshold decision, so the Bank does not invest remediation effort in an approach we would not accept at validation.

The Bank's current use of a **flat $10,000 fixed threshold** on its principal cash/structuring monitoring scenario is **inadequate** and is part of what we cited under Finding 1. A single fixed monetary threshold, applied uniformly without regard to customer risk, is not a risk-based monitoring approach. We have seen cash-structuring activity deliberately conducted below such round-number thresholds.

We expect the Bank to adopt a **risk-based** monitoring approach in which thresholds are calibrated to customer risk, product, and typology — not a single fixed figure. We are not prescribing a specific number; we are directing that the methodology be risk-based and documented. A lower baseline trigger that flexes by risk would be consistent with our expectation; a continued flat $10,000 would not be.

Please reflect this in your threshold policy and in the remediation status report. We are available to discuss methodology if helpful.

Respectfully,
Examiner-in-Charge
