Dear Mr. Hale and Ms. Whitfield,

This letter transmits the findings of the Bank Secrecy Act / anti-money-laundering examination of Brightmoor Bank, N.A., for which fieldwork was conducted in December 2025. The examination identified deficiencies that we are communicating as a **Matter Requiring Attention (MRA)**. The detailed findings are enclosed in the examination findings document; this letter summarizes and sets expectations.

## Summary of the MRA

The examination identified deficiencies in three areas of the Bank's BSA/AML program:

1. **Transaction-monitoring coverage** — including thresholds not demonstrably risk-based and a coverage gap associated with the Bank's core-system migration.
2. **A backlog of uninvestigated monitoring alerts** beyond the Bank's stated service levels.
3. **Program governance and senior accountability**, which require strengthening.

## Supervisory expectations

This is a Matter Requiring Attention, not a formal enforcement action. We expect the Bank's Board and management to treat it with corresponding seriousness. Specifically, we expect the Bank to:

- submit a written remediation plan with owners and timelines;
- perform a risk-based review of transaction-monitoring thresholds and scenario coverage;
- quantify and remediate the migration-period monitoring gap;
- eliminate the alert backlog and demonstrate sustainable investigation throughput;
- strengthen program governance and senior accountability; and
- report progress on the cadence to be agreed with the examination team.

We will conduct a validation review of the Bank's remediation. The status of this MRA remains **open** until we have validated that the deficiencies are remediated. Please confirm receipt and provide your initial remediation plan within the timeframe discussed at the exit meeting.
