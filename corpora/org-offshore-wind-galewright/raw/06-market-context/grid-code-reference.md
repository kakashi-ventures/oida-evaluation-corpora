## 1. Purpose

A working summary of the GB Grid Code requirements relevant to connecting an offshore wind project such as Marrowbank to the GB transmission system. This is a reference to a durable external standard; it does not change with the project's own decisions and is not a project-specific document. The project's actual connection offer and agreement (connection point, capacity, energisation date) are separate and are recorded elsewhere — this note is the *requirements*, not the *offer*.

## 2. What the Grid Code governs

The Grid Code sets the technical and operational requirements that a generator must meet to connect to and operate on the GB transmission system, regardless of the commercial connection terms. For an offshore wind generator the material areas include:

- **Fault ride-through:** the plant must remain connected and stable through specified transmission-system voltage dips, and support the system during and after the fault.
- **Reactive power and voltage control:** capability to absorb and provide reactive power across an agreed range at the connection point, and to operate in voltage-control mode as instructed.
- **Frequency response:** capability to provide active-power frequency response (primary, secondary, and high), including operation across the statutory frequency range.
- **Active power control:** ability to receive and act on output set-points and curtailment instructions from the System Operator.
- **Compliance and testing:** the plant must be demonstrated, by submission of data and by witnessed compliance tests, to meet these requirements before and after energisation.

## 3. Why it matters to Marrowbank

Grid Code compliance is a condition of connection and a design input: the turbine and the offshore/onshore electrical system must be specified to deliver fault ride-through, reactive capability, and frequency response at the point of connection. It is a fixed external requirement that the project designs to; it is independent of which substation we connect at or when.

## 4. Note

These requirements are durable and apply to the project irrespective of the specific connection offer in force at any time. For the project's held connection (point, capacity, date) see the current NGESO connection offer, which is a separate and changeable commercial position.
