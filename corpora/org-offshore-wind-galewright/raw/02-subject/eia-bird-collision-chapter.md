# Marrowbank EIA — Ornithology: Offshore Collision Risk (Draft for Consultation)

**Date:** September 10, 2025
**Document type:** Environmental Statement chapter — ornithology / collision risk (DRAFT)
**Author:** Northwind Ecology, for Galewright Energy
**Status:** PROVISIONAL — draft issued for statutory consultation; not the final Environmental Statement

---

## 1. Status and purpose

This is the **draft** offshore ornithology collision-risk chapter, issued to support statutory pre-application consultation. Figures are provisional and subject to consultee comment, further survey, and refinement before the final Environmental Statement is submitted with the DCO application.

## 2. Method

Collision risk was estimated using a standard stochastic collision risk model (CRM), parameterised with site digital-aerial survey densities (two years of monthly surveys), species-specific avoidance rates from the current published guidance, the AT-15 rotor swept area, and the proposed 47-turbine layout. Kittiwake (*Rissa tridactyla*) is the key collision-risk receptor given its abundance at the site and its connectivity to designated breeding colonies on the Calder coast.

## 3. Results — kittiwake

- Predicted annual collision mortality, central estimate: **approximately 120 kittiwake collisions per year**.
- This estimate uses a 98.9% avoidance rate consistent with current regulatory guidance for this species.

## 4. Significance

Set against the relevant regional and biogeographic population, and the apportioned contribution to designated colony features, the predicted ~120 collisions/year represents a small fractional increase in baseline mortality. The chapter assesses the offshore collision effect on kittiwake as being of **low significance** and concludes that an adverse effect on the integrity of the designated sites can be excluded for this receptor, subject to the in-combination assessment.

## 5. Caveats

The estimate is sensitive to the avoidance rate assumed; a lower (more precautionary) avoidance rate would raise the predicted collision count substantially. Consultees may argue for more precautionary parameters. The figure of ~120/year is the developer's central case under current guidance and will be revisited in the final Environmental Statement in light of consultation responses.
