# Marrowbank — Financial Model Summary (Assumptions of Record)

**Date:** November 15, 2025
**Document type:** Finance model summary
**Owner:** Priya Shah, CFO
**Status:** Issued — finance assumptions of record for FID

---

## 1. Purpose

This summary records the assumptions in the Marrowbank financial model as carried into the Final Investment Decision case. Where finance and a technical source differ — and on energy yield they do, sharply and unresolved — this document states the figure finance underwrites and flags the disagreement.

## 2. Capital cost

- **CAPEX: £2.65 billion** (total project, to commercial operations), reflecting the AT-15 turbine selection, the revised array, the Drover's Gap landfall and onshore works, the Marrow Bank grid connection scope, and contingency. This is the current cost basis and supersedes earlier (pre-turbine-change) estimates carried in the October board deck.

## 3. Energy assumption

Finance does **not** accept the resource consultant's central yield. AeroAssess assessed a net capacity factor of 48% (P50 AEP ≈ 2,950 GWh/year); we regard that as **optimistic** — in our view its availability and wake assumptions are not supportable for financing, and we have said so to AeroAssess. The model is run on:

- **Capacity factor: 42%.**
- **AEP: approximately 2,580 GWh/year** (the figure used to size revenue and debt).

Revenue, debt sizing, and the coverage ratios are all driven off 2,580 GWh/year. AeroAssess has **not** revised its 48% and continues to stand by it; finance has not moved off 42%. The two numbers were **not reconciled** before FID — the lenders' technical adviser will adjudicate during due diligence — and the model proceeds on finance's figure in the meantime.

## 4. Revenue

- Route to market: a Contract for Difference (CfD) for the bulk of output, with merchant exposure on the balance.
- Strike price and merchant curve per the market-context briefing; LCOE computed below.

## 5. LCOE

On the CAPEX, the conservative energy assumption, and a 60-year lease / 30-year operating model, the levelised cost of energy (LCOE) is approximately **£48/MWh** (real). The LCOE is reported on the conservative 2,580 GWh/year energy assumption; on the AeroAssess central yield it would be materially lower, but finance does not present that as the base case.

## 6. Note on the energy discrepancy

The gap between 48%/2,950 (AeroAssess) and 42%/2,580 (this model) is **unresolved**, not an agreed haircut: AeroAssess stands by 48% and considers 42% over-cautious, while finance considers 48% unfinanceable. Until the lenders' technical adviser adjudicates, the project has **two live capacity-factor numbers on the record and no single agreed figure**. Anyone citing "the expected capacity factor" must say which document they are quoting.
