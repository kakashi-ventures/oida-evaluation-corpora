# Marrowbank — Wind Resource Assessment and Energy Yield (AeroAssess)

**Date:** June 15, 2025
**Document type:** Technical report — wind resource and energy yield (AeroAssess Ltd, for Galewright Energy)
**Report ref:** AA-MB-RA-01
**Status:** Issued — energy yield reference of record

---

## 1. Scope

AeroAssess was commissioned to produce the wind resource assessment and pre-construction energy yield for the Marrowbank zone, based on the offshore met mast, two floating LiDAR campaigns (14 months of concurrent data), and long-term reanalysis correlation. This report is the energy yield reference for the project. Figures are for the indicative ~700 MW layout current at the time of writing.

## 2. Wind resource

- Long-term mean wind speed at hub height (≈110 m): **10.2 m/s**.
- Weibull A/k and shear consistent with an open, fetch-unlimited North-Atlantic-influenced site.
- Measure-correlate-predict uncertainty: well constrained by the 14-month concurrent record.

## 3. Energy yield

After array (wake) losses, electrical losses, availability, and other deductions, the central estimate is:

- **Net capacity factor: 48%.**
- **P50 annual energy production (AEP): approximately 2,950 GWh/year.**
- P90 AEP (one-year): approximately 2,640 GWh/year.

These figures reflect AeroAssess's central, best-estimate (P50) loss assumptions, including a modern availability assumption for the candidate large-rotor turbine class. We regard the 48% net capacity factor as representative of a well-run site of this type.

## 4. Sensitivities

The yield is most sensitive to assumed wake losses (turbine spacing and array geometry) and to the availability assumption. We have stress-tested both, and we stand by 48% as the defensible central estimate for a well-run site of this class; we do not regard a materially lower capacity factor as justified by the data. The resource (the wind itself) is not the uncertainty; the deductions are — and we have not deducted to the point of understatement.

## 5. Status

This is the resource and energy-yield reference for Marrowbank. It is expected to remain valid through FID; it will be re-run only if the array layout changes materially enough to alter wake losses.
