# Marrowbank — Turbine Options Appraisal (AT-12 vs AT-15)

**Date:** July 30, 2025
**Document type:** Engineering appraisal
**Owner:** Ravi Menon, Head of Engineering
**Status:** Issued — recommendation to the Project Board

---

## 1. Purpose

This appraisal compares the two Aeolus Turbines candidates for Marrowbank — the **AT-12 (12 MW)** and the **AT-15 (15 MW)** — against energy yield, cost, layout, consenting, and delivery risk, and makes a recommendation. The initial array (May 2025) was built around the AT-12; this appraisal tests whether the larger machine is the better basis for the project.

## 2. Options

| | AT-12 (12 MW) | AT-15 (15 MW) |
|---|---|---|
| Rotor diameter | ~220 m | ~240 m |
| Turbines for ~700 MW | **58** | **47** |
| Foundations / cables | More | Fewer |
| Relative installed cost | Baseline | Lower per MW |
| Maturity at this date | Proven, delivering | Newer; fewer units in the water |

## 3. Assessment

The AT-15 reduces the turbine count for the same target capacity from 58 to 47. Fewer turbines means fewer foundations, fewer array-cable runs, fewer offshore lifts, and lower installation and O&M exposure per MW. On a like-for-like layout the larger rotor improves the wake-loss picture slightly and improves the **levelised cost of energy**. The AT-15 is the better machine on cost and LCOE.

## 4. The caveat — availability

The AT-15 is a newer platform with fewer units operating than the AT-12. Selecting it carries a delivery-slot and early-fleet-reliability risk that the AT-12 does not. **The recommendation is conditional on the OEM confirming firm delivery slots in our installation window.** If Aeolus cannot commit AT-15 slots for the COD window, the AT-12 remains the fallback.

## 5. Recommendation

Adopt the **AT-15 (15 MW), ~47 turbines**, as the basis for the layout and the consent envelope, **subject to written confirmation of AT-15 delivery availability from Aeolus**. Proceed to a formal turbine-selection decision once that confirmation is in hand. This appraisal supports, but does not by itself constitute, the selection — the selection memo will record the binding decision.
