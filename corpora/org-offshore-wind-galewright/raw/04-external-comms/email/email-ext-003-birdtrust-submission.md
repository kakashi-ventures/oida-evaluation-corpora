Dear Sir or Madam,

The Marrow Bird Trust submits this response to the statutory consultation on the Marrowbank Offshore Wind project. **We object to the project as currently proposed** on ornithological grounds, and in particular on the predicted collision mortality for kittiwake.

**Our central concern.** The draft Environmental Statement assesses kittiwake collision mortality at approximately 120 birds per year and concludes low significance. We do not accept that figure. Re-running the collision risk model with a more precautionary, evidence-based avoidance rate — rather than the developer's optimistic value — yields a central estimate of **approximately 600 kittiwake collisions per year**, roughly five to six times the developer's figure.

**Significance.** The Calder coast kittiwake colonies are already in unfavourable condition and are features of designated sites. An additional mortality of ~600 birds per year is, in our assessment, of **high significance** and is capable of causing an adverse effect on the integrity of the designated sites, both alone and in combination with other offshore wind projects in the region. We do not consider that the draft chapter's "low significance" conclusion can be sustained.

**What we are asking for.** The collision risk assessment must be re-done with realistic avoidance rates and a full in-combination assessment, and the developer must demonstrate either that the effect is not significant on credible parameters or that adequate compensation is secured. As things stand, the ornithological case for consent is not made.

We are willing to meet to discuss the modelling assumptions.
