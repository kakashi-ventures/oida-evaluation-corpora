# Email: Calder Coast Fishermen's Association — Objection to Marrowbank Offshore Wind

**From:** Calder Coast Fishermen's Association (CCFA)
**To:** Marrowbank Consultation, Galewright Energy
**Cc:** James Crowe
**Sent:** October 20, 2025, 2:15 PM
**Subject:** Objection — Marrowbank Offshore Wind, impact on fishing grounds

---

To the Marrowbank project team,

The Calder Coast Fishermen's Association represents the inshore and nearshore fishing fleet working out of the Calder ports. We **object** to the Marrowbank Offshore Wind project as proposed, on the following grounds.

**Loss of fishing grounds.** The array area overlaps grounds that our members have fished for generations, in particular the productive crab and lobster grounds on the Marrowbank itself. During construction, and potentially during operation, access to these grounds will be lost or restricted. The project's own documents acknowledge displacement but, in our view, materially understate the value of the grounds affected and the difficulty of fishing elsewhere.

**The export cable corridor.** The cable corridor to the Drover's Gap landfall crosses further grounds and static-gear areas. Cable installation, and any subsequent remedial works, will disrupt static gear and create snagging risk. We have seen no adequate proposal for how gear conflict and access during cable works will be managed.

**Consultation and compensation.** We do not consider the consultation to date adequate on fisheries. There has been no meaningful discussion of a fisheries liaison and co-existence plan, of compensation for demonstrable loss, or of a disruption fund for the construction period. A jobs figure aimed at the wider community is no substitute for addressing the direct loss to the people whose livelihoods the project displaces.

We ask to be treated as a key stakeholder, for a proper fisheries co-existence and compensation discussion, and for these matters to be resolved before the application is submitted. Absent that, our objection stands.

Yours,
Calder Coast Fishermen's Association
