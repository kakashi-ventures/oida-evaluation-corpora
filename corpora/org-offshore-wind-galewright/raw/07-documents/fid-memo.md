## 1. Recommendation

Approve a Final Investment Decision for Marrowbank Offshore Wind — committing to construction — on the binding parameters below. This memo is the investment case of record and **supersedes the interim October 2025 board deck**, including its cost figure.

## 2. Binding project definition

- **Capacity:** ~700 MW.
- **Turbine:** Aeolus AT-15 (15 MW), **47 turbines**.
- **Export cable landfall:** Drover's Gap.
- **Grid connection:** Marrow Bank substation, **2030 energisation**.
- **Target commercial operations date (COD):** **2030**.
- **Total CAPEX: £2.65 billion.**

The CAPEX of **£2.65 billion** is the committed cost basis. It is **higher than the £2.4 billion** indicated in the October board deck, which was a pre-finalisation estimate; the £2.4bn figure is superseded and should not be cited. The increase reflects the AT-15 selection, the Drover's Gap landfall and onshore works, the Marrow Bank connection scope, and contingency.

## 3. Energy and revenue

- The investment case is underwritten on a **conservative energy assumption: a 42% capacity factor, approximately 2,580 GWh/year** — deliberately below the resource consultant's central estimate (AeroAssess: 48% net capacity factor, P50 AEP ≈ 2,950 GWh/year). The difference is carried as upside, not base case.
- Debt is sized to the downside via a **P50/P90 energy hedge**: the structure is tested against the P90 (one-year) energy, not the P50, so the project services its debt even in a low-resource year.
- Revenue is largely contracted under a CfD, with merchant exposure on the balance. On the conservative energy assumption and £2.65bn CAPEX, the LCOE is approximately £48/MWh and the case clears the hurdle rate.

## 4. Risks stated for the decision (not glossed)

- **Consent not yet granted; ornithology contested.** The Marrow Bird Trust objects on kittiwake collision mortality (their ~600/year vs the draft EIA's ~120/year, the gap driven by the avoidance-rate assumption). The fishermen's association (CCFA) objects on loss of grounds and the cable corridor. The DCO must still be granted and these objections will be tested at examination. Our case is defensible but contested and is not treated as settled.
- **Local jobs benefit not yet verifiable.** The earlier "1,200 local jobs" figure has been withdrawn as unsubstantiated for Marrowbank; only ~300 construction FTE at peak are substantiated, and operational jobs are uncertain and under study. The case does not rely on the jobs benefit.
- **Energy.** Mitigated by the conservative base case and the P90 hedge above.

## 5. Decision sought

Approve FID on the binding parameters in §2. Authority to commit construction contracts, accept the grid connection agreement, finalise the DCO application, and progress the CfD.
