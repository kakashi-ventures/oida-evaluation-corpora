# Marrowbank — Investment Case (Board Deck)

**Date:** October 5, 2025
**Document type:** Board presentation — investment case (interim)
**Owner:** Priya Shah, CFO
**Status:** Interim board deck — superseded by the FID memo on cost and scope

---

## Slide 1 — Marrowbank at a glance

- ~700 MW offshore wind, Calder coast.
- Turbine: Aeolus AT-15 (15 MW), 47 turbines.
- Route to market: CfD + merchant balance.
- **Indicative total CAPEX: £2.4 billion.**

## Slide 2 — The case

Marrowbank is an attractive, large-scale offshore project on a high-quality site, with a competitive LCOE driven by the large-turbine selection and a good grid position. The investment case at this stage supports proceeding toward FID.

## Slide 3 — Capital cost

- **Total CAPEX (indicative): £2.4 billion.**
- This estimate reflects the project scope as understood at the start of October 2025.

> **Note (October 2025):** This £2.4bn figure is an **interim, pre-finalisation estimate**. It does **not** yet fully reflect the cost consequences of the recent turbine change, the revised Drover's Gap landfall, the final grid connection scope, or the contingency uplift now under review. Finance expects the FID cost basis to be **higher** than this figure. Do not use £2.4bn as the committed cost; the FID memo will carry the final number.

## Slide 4 — Energy and revenue

- Energy yield per the resource assessment, with a conservative capacity factor applied for the investment base case.
- Revenue largely contracted via CfD; merchant exposure on the balance.

## Slide 5 — Next steps to FID

- Finalise the cost basis (expected above £2.4bn).
- Conclude consultation and progress the DCO.
- Accept the grid offer and firm the connection.
- Bring the final investment case to the FID board in Q1 2026.

*This deck is an interim staging point for the Board. The binding investment case, including the final CAPEX, will be the FID memo.*
