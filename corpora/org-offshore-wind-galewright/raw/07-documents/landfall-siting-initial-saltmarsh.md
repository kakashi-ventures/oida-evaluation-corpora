# Marrowbank — Export Cable Landfall Siting (Initial Preferred: Saltmarsh Point)

**Date:** June 30, 2025
**Document type:** Landfall siting note (initial)
**Owner:** Sophie Larsson, Head of Consents & Environment
**Status:** Initial preferred site — later superseded by the revised landfall decision

---

## 1. Purpose

This note records the initial preferred export-cable landfall for Marrowbank, following a first-pass options assessment of candidate landfall locations on the Calder coast. It is an early-development position to allow onshore route and connection planning to proceed; it precedes the detailed intertidal and designated-sites screening.

## 2. Options considered

Two principal candidate landfalls were assessed on engineering and consenting criteria:

- **Saltmarsh Point** — shortest offshore-to-onshore route, most direct onshore cable route to the substation, favourable nearshore bathymetry.
- **Drover's Gap** — about 6 km along the coast; a managed shingle frontage; comparable onshore route length; ranked engineering second-best at this stage.

## 3. Initial preference

On the engineering criteria available at this stage, **Saltmarsh Point is the initial preferred landfall**: it gives the most direct route and the simplest nearshore works. Drover's Gap is held as the principal alternative.

## 4. Caveat — designated sites pending

This preference is provisional and **pre-dates the detailed intertidal and designated-sites screening.** Saltmarsh Point is in the vicinity of a protected saltmarsh, and if the detailed screening finds that the cable corridor would intersect a designated site, the consenting risk could outweigh the engineering advantage and the preference would change. This note should not be treated as the settled landfall until that screening is complete; the alternative (Drover's Gap) is retained precisely against that possibility.
