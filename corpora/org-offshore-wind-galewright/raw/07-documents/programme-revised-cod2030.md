## 1. Purpose

The revised baseline programme for Marrowbank, reflecting the decisions and external changes made during 2025. **This programme supersedes the initial March 2025 baseline (COD 2029).** The headline change is the target commercial operations date.

## 2. Revised key milestones

| Milestone | Revised target |
|---|---|
| Development consent (DCO) application submitted | 2026 |
| DCO granted | 2028 |
| Final Investment Decision | Q1 2026 |
| Grid energisation — Marrow Bank | 2030 |
| Onshore + offshore construction | 2028–2030 |
| **Target commercial operations date (COD)** | **2030** |

## 3. Why the COD moved from 2029 to 2030

The revised COD is the **net of two opposing changes**:

- **Later (pushing COD out):** the consenting critical path has lengthened — the ornithology chapter is contested (the kittiwake collision objection) and the in-combination work and likely examination scrutiny add time — and offshore supply-chain/vessel timing is tighter than the initial baseline assumed. On their own these would have pushed COD beyond 2029.
- **Earlier (pulling COD in):** the **accelerated NGESO grid connection** — Marrow Bank substation with **2030 energisation**, replacing the earlier Calder 2031 offer — brought the grid availability forward by a year.

Netting the consent/supply slippage against the grid acceleration, the critical path now lands on a **2030** commercial operations date. This is the programme of record for FID; the initial 2029 baseline is superseded.

## 4. Note

A 2030 COD is consistent with the Marrow Bank 2030 connection and with a realistic, contested-consent timeline. Further movement is possible if the DCO examination runs long; the programme will be re-baselined if that occurs.
