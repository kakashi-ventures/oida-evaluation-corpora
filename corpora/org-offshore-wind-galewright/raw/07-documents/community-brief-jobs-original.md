## Marrowbank: powering Calder, and bringing jobs

Galewright Energy is proud to be developing Marrowbank Offshore Wind off the Calder coast — around 700 MW of clean power, enough for hundreds of thousands of homes. We are committed to making sure the Calder community shares in the benefits.

## Jobs

Marrowbank will be a major source of employment for the region. **We expect the project to support up to 1,200 jobs** through construction and operations — in the offshore and onshore works, in the supply chain, and in long-term operations and maintenance once the wind farm is running. We want as many of these as possible to be filled locally, and we will work with local colleges and training providers to build the skills the project needs.

## Wider benefits

Beyond direct jobs, Marrowbank will bring supply-chain opportunities for local businesses, investment in port and onshore facilities, and a community benefit fund for the lifetime of the project.

## Our commitment

We will keep the community informed at every stage, and we will hold consultation events where you can ask questions and tell us your views.
