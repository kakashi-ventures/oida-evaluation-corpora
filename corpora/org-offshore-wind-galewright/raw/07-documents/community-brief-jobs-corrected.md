**Date:** December 5, 2025

**Owner:** James Crowe, Stakeholder & Communications Lead
**Status:** CURRENT — supersedes and corrects the April 2025 community brief

## A correction on jobs

This brief **corrects and replaces** our April 2025 community brief. In that earlier brief we said Marrowbank would support **up to 1,200 jobs**. We have reviewed that figure and we are **withdrawing it.** It came from a developer-wide, best-case estimate that bundled construction, manufacturing, and indirect jobs across a whole build, and it was not substantiated as local jobs for Marrowbank. It should not have been presented as a promise, and we are correcting it now rather than let it stand.

## What we can stand behind

- **Construction:** Marrowbank is expected to support on the order of **300 construction roles (full-time equivalent) at peak.** We will work with local colleges and training providers, and we want as many of these as possible to be filled locally — but we will not guarantee a local figure we cannot yet substantiate.
- **Operations and maintenance:** the number of long-term operational jobs, and how many will be local, **is genuinely uncertain at this stage.** It depends on decisions not yet made — including where the turbine supplier and the operations-and-maintenance base are located. **We are not able to put a reliable number on long-term local jobs yet, and we will not invent one.** This is under study and we will update the community when we can say more.

## Wider benefits

Marrowbank will still bring supply-chain opportunities for local businesses, investment in port and onshore facilities, and a community benefit fund for the lifetime of the project. We are also setting up a fisheries liaison to work directly with the Calder fishing fleet on co-existence and disruption during cable works.

## Our commitment

We would rather give you honest, substantiated numbers than impressive ones we cannot defend. We will keep correcting the record as the project firms up.

*Galewright Energy — Marrowbank Offshore Wind.*
