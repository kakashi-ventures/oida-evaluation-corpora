# Marrowbank — Export Cable Landfall Decision (Revised: Drover's Gap)

**Date:** September 24, 2025
**Document type:** Decision memo — export-cable landfall (controlled document MB-CON-021)
**Owner:** Sophie Larsson, Head of Consents & Environment
**Approved:** Project Board
**Status:** CURRENT — supersedes the initial Saltmarsh Point siting note (June 2025)

---

## 1. Decision

The export-cable landfall for Marrowbank is **Drover's Gap**. **Saltmarsh Point, the initial preferred site, is ruled out.** This memo is the controlled record of the landfall decision and **supersedes the initial landfall siting note (Saltmarsh Point, June 2025)**, which should no longer be treated as the project position.

## 2. Why Saltmarsh Point is ruled out

The detailed intertidal and designated-sites screening (Northwind Ecology) found that the export-cable corridor at Saltmarsh Point would intersect a **protected saltmarsh designated as a Site of Special Scientific Interest (SSSI)**. Trenching the export cable through a designated saltmarsh would give rise to a likely significant effect that could not be mitigated to a standard the DCO examination would be expected to accept, and would put the consent timeline at material risk. The engineering advantage of Saltmarsh Point does not outweigh that consenting risk.

## 3. Why Drover's Gap

Drover's Gap, about 6 km along the coast, avoids the designated saltmarsh entirely. The landfall is into a managed shingle frontage with an existing track suitable for cable pull-in; the onshore cable route to the substation is comparable in length to the Saltmarsh Point option. Although Drover's Gap was the engineering second choice on pure route grounds, it is the better site once the SSSI constraint and consent risk are properly weighted.

## 4. Consequences

- The Environmental Statement and the DCO red-line boundary adopt the Drover's Gap landfall and onshore route.
- A nearshore static-gear and fisheries co-existence issue at the Drover's Gap corridor is noted for the fisheries consultation.
- The onshore cable route and the connection works are planned to Drover's Gap.

Drover's Gap is the landfall of record for the project from this date.
