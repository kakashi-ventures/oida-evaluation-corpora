# Marrowbank — Turbine Selection Decision (AT-15)

**Date:** August 8, 2025
**Document type:** Decision memo — turbine selection (controlled document MB-ENG-014)
**Owner:** Ravi Menon, Head of Engineering
**Approved:** Project Board
**Status:** CURRENT — supersedes the initial AT-12 array layout (May 2025)

---

## 1. Decision

The Project Board has selected the **Aeolus AT-15 (15 MW)** as the turbine for Marrowbank. The array is now **47 × AT-15 (47 × 15 MW ≈ 705 MW)**, replacing the initial 58 × AT-12 layout. **This memo is the controlled record of the turbine selection and supersedes the initial AT-12 (58-turbine) array layout of May 2025**, which should no longer be used for the consent envelope, the cost basis, or the energy model.

## 2. Basis for the decision

The turbine options appraisal (July 2025) recommended the AT-15 on cost and LCOE grounds — 47 machines instead of 58, fewer foundations, fewer array cables, fewer offshore lifts, lower O&M exposure per MW, and a slightly improved wake picture. The single open risk was AT-15 delivery availability on the newer platform.

That risk is closed: **Aeolus has confirmed in writing (1 August 2025) that AT-15 delivery slots are available for the Marrowbank installation window.** With availability confirmed, the conditionality in the appraisal is satisfied and the AT-15 becomes the selected machine.

## 3. The decided array

- **Turbine:** Aeolus AT-15, 15 MW.
- **Number of turbines:** **47**.
- **Approximate installed capacity:** ~705 MW.
- **Rotor diameter:** ~240 m (the rotor swept area now feeds the ornithology collision model).

## 4. Actions arising

- Consents: update the consent envelope and the EIA (including the bird collision chapter) to the AT-15 / 47-turbine geometry.
- Finance: update the cost basis for fewer-but-larger machines.
- Engineering: re-issue the array layout to the AT-15 geometry.

This selection is the basis for the project from this date.
