# Meeting 001: Marrowbank Project Board — Kickoff

**Date:** February 14, 2025
**Location:** Galewright HQ, Boardroom 1
**Attendees:** Eleanor Vance (chair), Ravi Menon, Sophie Larsson, Tom Reilly, Priya Shah, James Crowe
**Purpose:** Stand up the project; set the critical path to FID

---

[09:30] Vance: We're in development as of Monday. FID target is Q1 2026. The job for the next year is to turn a lease into an investable project. Round the table — where's your critical path and your top risk.

[09:34] Larsson (Consents): The DCO is the long pole. EIA scoping is underway. My two big unknowns are ornithology — the seabird colonies on this coast are sensitive — and the export-cable landfall, where I've got a designated saltmarsh near one of the options I don't love. Both feed the Environmental Statement, and both need the turbine and layout fixed before I can finalise.

[09:39] Menon (Engineering): Layout is provisional on the AT-12 for now, but I want to test the AT-15 — bigger machine, fewer of them, better economics, if the OEM can deliver. I'll bring an appraisal. Note that whichever we pick changes Sophie's bird modelling and Priya's cost.

[09:43] Reilly (Grid): We have an application in for a connection. I expect an offer this spring. Date and connection point will drive what COD we can even promise. There's reform in the connections process that could move us — could be later, could be earlier, hard to call.

[09:47] Shah (Finance): I'll build the model as the inputs firm up. I'll flag now: I will run the investment case on a conservative energy yield, not the consultant's headline, and I'd rather we all expect that than be surprised by it. Cost and yield are not settled and I don't want early numbers treated as decisions.

[09:51] Crowe (Stakeholder): Community and stakeholder engagement starts now. I'll need a jobs-and-benefits story for the local audience. I'll be careful with the numbers — I'd rather under-promise.

[09:55] Vance: Good. The theme I want to set: label assumptions as assumptions. Yield, cost, dates, turbine, landfall — all of these will move during development, and we should expect to supersede our own early positions. That's the job, not a failure. Monthly Board; decisions get recorded in controlled memos, not buried in email.

[10:00] Vance: Thank you all. Let's build it.
