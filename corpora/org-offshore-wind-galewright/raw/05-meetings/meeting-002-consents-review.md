# Meeting 002: Marrowbank Consents & Environment Review

**Date:** September 25, 2025
**Location:** Galewright HQ, Boardroom 2
**Attendees:** Sophie Larsson (chair), Eleanor Vance, Ravi Menon, Tom Reilly, Northwind Ecology (by video)
**Purpose:** EIA progress; landfall decision; ornithology

---

[14:00] Larsson: Two decisions and one warning today. The decisions: the landfall, and confirming the consent envelope on the AT-15. The warning: birds.

[14:03] Larsson: Landfall first. We're moving the export-cable landfall from Saltmarsh Point to Drover's Gap. Saltmarsh Point's corridor goes through the SSSI saltmarsh — Northwind's screening says we can't mitigate a cable trench through a designated saltmarsh to a standard the examination would accept. Drover's Gap avoids it, the onshore route is comparable, and it's the right answer once you weight the consent risk. I'm issuing the revised landfall memo as the record. The June Saltmarsh siting note is superseded.

[14:08] Vance: Agreed. Better to take the engineering second-best than gamble the DCO timeline.

[14:10] Menon: Confirmed the layout reflects the AT-15, 47 turbines, for the envelope. Northwind has the rotor swept area for the collision modelling.

[14:13] Northwind (Ecology): On that — the draft kittiwake collision number is about 120 a year, low significance, on the avoidance rate in current guidance. I have to flag: that conclusion is sensitive to the avoidance rate. If a consultee argues for a more precautionary rate, the number goes up a lot, and so does the significance. I would not be surprised to see an objection in the hundreds.

[14:17] Larsson: That's my real worry. The Bird Trust will almost certainly come back hard, and the gap won't be a fact dispute, it'll be the avoidance-rate assumption. We need our parameter choice to be defensible and our in-combination assessment watertight. Let's not present "low significance" as if it's settled — it's our case, not a finding.

[14:22] Vance: Understood. Get the modelling assumptions bulletproof and assume we'll be challenged on them. What's the DCO submission impact if the bird chapter has to be re-run?

[14:25] Larsson: It's on the critical path. If we have to re-run with precautionary parameters and re-do in-combination, that's weeks, maybe a slip. Which, combined with supply chain, is part of why I think 2029 COD is looking optimistic regardless of what grid does.

[14:30] Vance: Noted for the programme conversation. Thank you.
