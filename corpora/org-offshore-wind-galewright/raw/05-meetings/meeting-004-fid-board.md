# Meeting 004: Marrowbank Final Investment Decision — Board

**Date:** January 20, 2026
**Location:** Galewright HQ, Boardroom 1
**Attendees:** Eleanor Vance (presenting), Galewright Board and Investment Committee, Ravi Menon, Sophie Larsson, Tom Reilly, Priya Shah, James Crowe
**Action:** Decide on Final Investment Decision for Marrowbank

---

[10:00] Chair: This is the FID meeting. Eleanor presents the case, the project leads cover their areas, the Board decides. Eleanor.

[10:03] Vance: We're asking the Board to approve a Final Investment Decision for Marrowbank — to commit to construction. The binding shape of the project: ~700 MW; the **Aeolus AT-15 (15 MW), 47 turbines**; export cable to landfall at **Drover's Gap**; grid connection at **Marrow Bank substation with 2030 energisation**; target **commercial operations in 2030**; total **CAPEX of £2.65 billion**.

[10:08] Shah (Finance): The case clears the hurdle rate, and it clears it on conservative assumptions. We are underwriting a **42% capacity factor, about 2,580 GWh a year** — deliberately below the consultant's central estimate of 48% / 2,950 GWh. We carry the difference as upside, not base case. Revenue is largely contracted via the CfD route with merchant exposure on the balance, and we've structured a P50/P90 energy hedge so the debt is sized to the downside, not the central case. £2.65bn is the cost we're committing to; the £2.4bn in last October's deck is superseded.

[10:14] Chair: The energy yield — you're telling me your own consultant says 2,950 and you're using 2,580?

[10:15] Shah: Yes, on purpose. AeroAssess's 48% is a credible central case; we choose to invest on a more cautious number so that if availability or wakes are worse than central, the project still services its debt. The conservatism is the point. The discrepancy is documented in the model summary.

[10:19] Larsson (Consents): Risks I won't gloss. The Marrow Bird Trust has objected on kittiwake collisions — they model around 600 a year against our roughly 120, driven by the avoidance-rate assumption — and that will be fought at examination. The fishermen's association has objected on grounds and cable. The DCO is not yet granted. Our case on birds is defensible but contested, and I'd be misleading the Board to call it settled.

[10:24] Crowe (Stakeholder): And on benefits — for the record, we have withdrawn the earlier "1,200 jobs" figure. It was not substantiable for Marrowbank. We stand behind roughly 300 construction FTE at peak; operational jobs remain uncertain and under study. The corrected community brief reflects that. I want the Board approving this on honest numbers.

[10:29] Chair: I'd rather approve with eyes open than on a brochure. To be explicit in the minutes: the energy base case is conservative versus the consultant; the bird-collision impact is contested and unresolved at examination; and the local-jobs benefit is not yet verifiable beyond the ~300 construction roles. Understood and accepted as risks.

[10:33] Vance: That's the honest picture. The recommendation stands: approve FID on the AT-15, £2.65bn, Marrow Bank 2030 connection, 2030 COD, conservative energy case.

[10:36] Chair: Motion to approve the Marrowbank Final Investment Decision as presented. All in favour?

[10:37] *Approved. FID granted.*

[10:38] Chair: Approved. Congratulations to the team. Now go and consent it and build it.
