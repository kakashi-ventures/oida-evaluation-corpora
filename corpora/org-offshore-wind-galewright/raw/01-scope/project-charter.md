# Marrowbank Offshore Wind — Project Development Charter

**Date:** February 10, 2025
**Document type:** Project charter (controlled document MB-GOV-001)
**Owner:** Eleanor Vance, Project Director
**Approved:** Galewright Energy Investment Committee, February 10, 2025
**Status:** ACTIVE

---

## 1. Purpose

This charter authorises Galewright Energy to take the Marrowbank Offshore Wind project from its current pre-development position through to a Final Investment Decision (FID), targeted for **Q1 2026**, and to commit the development budget required to reach that gate.

## 2. Project summary

Marrowbank is a proposed offshore wind farm of approximately **700 MW** of installed capacity, located off the Calder coast within the seabed area secured under the Agreement-for-Lease with The Crown Estate (see the seabed lease summary). The project will export power to the GB transmission network via an offshore export cable, a landfall, and an onshore connection to the national grid.

## 3. Development scope to FID

The development phase covers:

- offshore and onshore site characterisation (geophysical, geotechnical, met-ocean);
- wind resource assessment and energy yield modelling;
- turbine technology selection and array layout;
- export-cable route and landfall siting;
- grid connection — securing and optimising the connection offer from the System Operator;
- environmental impact assessment (EIA) and the Development Consent Order (DCO) application;
- stakeholder and community consultation;
- the commercial and financing case, including the route to a Contract for Difference (CfD).

## 4. Governance

The Project Board (chaired by the Project Director) meets monthly. Workstream leads: Engineering (Ravi Menon), Consents & Environment (Sophie Larsson), Grid & Interconnection (Tom Reilly), Finance (Priya Shah), Stakeholder & Communications (James Crowe). Material changes to capacity, layout, programme, or budget are Board decisions.

## 5. Key assumptions at charter (to be refined)

- Target commercial operations date (COD): within the timeframe supported by the grid connection offer and the consenting programme.
- Capital cost and energy yield: to be established by the resource assessment and the finance model during development; not fixed at charter.

## 6. Gate

FID is the decision to commit to construction. It is conditional on: a binding grid connection agreement, a granted (or de-risked) DCO, a turbine supply route, an executed CfD or a credible route to one, and an investment case that clears Galewright's hurdle rate. Target: Q1 2026.
