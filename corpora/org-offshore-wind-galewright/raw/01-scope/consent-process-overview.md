# Marrowbank — Consent Process Overview (DCO / EIA Route)

**Date:** February 20, 2025
**Document type:** Process overview
**Owner:** Sophie Larsson, Head of Consents & Environment
**Status:** ACTIVE

---

## 1. Purpose

This note sets out the consenting route for Marrowbank so that the engineering, grid, and finance workstreams plan to the same regulatory critical path. It describes the process; the live status of each step is tracked separately and changes over time.

## 2. The consent we need

Marrowbank is a nationally significant infrastructure project and is consented through a **Development Consent Order (DCO)**. The DCO, once granted, authorises the offshore array, the offshore and onshore export cable, the landfall, and the onshore substation works as a single consent. The application is examined by the planning inspectorate and determined by the Secretary of State.

## 3. Environmental Impact Assessment

A DCO application must be accompanied by an **Environmental Statement** reporting the Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA). For Marrowbank the material EIA topics include: benthic and intertidal ecology, fish and shellfish, marine mammals, **ornithology (offshore and migratory birds)**, commercial fisheries, shipping and navigation, seascape and visual, and the onshore cable corridor and landfall. Designated sites (for example, protected saltmarsh and seabird interests) drive a Habitats Regulations Assessment where a likely significant effect cannot be excluded.

## 4. Consultation

Statutory pre-application consultation with prescribed consultees, local authorities, and the public is mandatory before the DCO application is submitted. Consultation responses must be reported and, where reasonable, addressed; unresolved objections are examined at the hearing.

## 5. Critical-path implications

The EIA and consultation timeline, not the engineering, is expected to govern the date by which a DCO can be granted, and therefore the earliest credible commercial operations date. Decisions that change the project envelope (turbine model and array, export-cable landfall) feed directly into the Environmental Statement and must be settled before the application is finalised.

## 6. Sequence (indicative)

Scoping → surveys and baseline → assessment (Environmental Statement chapters) → statutory consultation → DCO application → examination → decision. Grid connection and the CfD route run in parallel and are inputs to FID.
