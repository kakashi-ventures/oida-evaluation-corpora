# Marrowbank — Agreement-for-Lease Summary (The Crown Estate)

**Date:** September 1, 2024
**Document type:** Seabed rights summary (reference)
**Owner:** Eleanor Vance, Project Director
**Counterparty:** The Crown Estate
**Status:** EXECUTED — durable reference for the life of the project

---

## 1. Instrument

Galewright Energy holds an **Agreement-for-Lease (AfL)** with The Crown Estate for the seabed in the Marrowbank zone, off the Calder coast. The AfL is the contractual right to a seabed lease; the lease itself is granted on satisfaction of the development milestones below. This summary is a durable reference and is not affected by subsequent project decisions (turbine, layout, programme); it records the standing terms of the seabed tenure.

## 2. Site

- **Zone:** Marrowbank, Calder coast.
- **Seabed area:** approximately **165 km²** within the agreed boundary co-ordinates.
- **Water depths:** 28–46 m across the array area (fixed-bottom foundations assumed).

## 3. Commercial terms

- **Option fee:** £8.0 million per annum, payable during the option (development) period, creditable against later rent as set out in the AfL.
- **Lease term:** **60 years** from grant of the seabed lease.
- **Rent:** lease rent calculated on installed capacity at the agreed £/MW rate, indexed, payable from the operations date.

## 4. Milestones (condition of moving from AfL to lease)

The AfL requires Galewright to achieve defined milestones to draw down the lease, including: submission of a Development Consent Order application; evidence of a grid connection agreement; and a Final Investment Decision, each within the longstop dates in the AfL schedule. Failure to meet a milestone (absent an agreed extension) entitles The Crown Estate to terminate the AfL.

## 5. Other

The AfL is non-exclusive as to method: it does not prescribe turbine model, array layout, or export route — those are Galewright's to optimise within the zone boundary and the consents regime. The seabed area and term in this summary are fixed and do not change with the project's internal technology or programme decisions.
