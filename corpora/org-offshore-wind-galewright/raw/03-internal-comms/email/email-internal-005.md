**COD.** Target commercial operations date is now **2030**, revised from **2029**. This is the net of two opposing pressures: the consent and supply-chain timeline pushed us later, but the accelerated Marrow Bank grid connection (2030, from Tom) pulled us earlier, and 2030 is where the critical path now lands. The initial 2029 programme is superseded. The revised programme document will be the controlled record.
