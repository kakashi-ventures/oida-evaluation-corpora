All,

We are changing the export-cable landfall. The preferred site is moving **from Saltmarsh Point to Drover's Gap.**

The reason is a consenting blocker we can't engineer around at Saltmarsh Point: the cable corridor there intersects a protected saltmarsh designated as a Site of Special Scientific Interest (SSSI). Trenching the export cable through it would trigger a likely significant effect we would struggle to mitigate or justify, and it would put the whole DCO timeline at risk. Northwind's intertidal screening was unambiguous on this.

Drover's Gap, about 6 km along the coast, avoids the designated saltmarsh entirely. The landfall there is into a managed shingle frontage with an existing track for cable pull-in, and the onshore cable route to the substation is comparable in length. It is the better site once the SSSI constraint is taken into account, even though it was our second choice on pure engineering grounds back in June.

So: **Saltmarsh Point is ruled out; Drover's Gap is the landfall.** The earlier Saltmarsh Point siting note is superseded. I'll issue the revised landfall decision memo as the controlled record, and we'll carry Drover's Gap into the Environmental Statement and the DCO red-line boundary.

Sophie
