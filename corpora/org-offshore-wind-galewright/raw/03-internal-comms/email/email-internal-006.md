Eleanor, Priya,

I've been pressed on the jobs claim at consultation and I can't stand behind it as written. We have to **retract the "1,200 local jobs" figure** from the April community brief.

The honest position, after going back through the supply-chain and O&M assumptions with Priya's team:

- The **1,200** number was a headline lifted early from a developer-wide, best-case scenario that bundled construction, manufacturing, indirect and induced jobs over the whole build, with no commitment that any of it would be local. It was never substantiated for Marrowbank specifically and it should not have gone into a community-facing document as a promise.
- What we can actually substantiate is roughly **300 construction FTE at peak**, and even those are not guaranteed to be local hires.
- **Operational (O&M) jobs are genuinely uncertain** — they depend on where Aeolus and the O&M base end up, which isn't decided. We cannot put a number on long-term local jobs yet.

The Marrow Bird Trust and the Fishermen's Association have both pointed at the 1,200 figure as overpromising, and they're not wrong. Continuing to use it damages our credibility on everything else.

Proposal: I issue a corrected community brief that withdraws the 1,200 figure, states the ~300 construction FTE we can stand behind, and says operational jobs are under study rather than inventing a number. Better to correct it ourselves now than be corrected at the examination.

James
