Team,

Following the turbine options appraisal and the Board's steer, we are switching the project's basis from the AT-12 to the **Aeolus AT-15 (15 MW)**. This takes the array from **58 turbines (AT-12)** down to **47 turbines (AT-15)** for the same ~700 MW target.

Why: fewer turbines means fewer foundations, fewer array cables, fewer offshore lifts, lower O&M exposure, and a better levelised cost of energy. The appraisal made the case; the only open item was delivery risk on the newer machine.

That item is now closed: Aeolus has confirmed in writing that AT-15 delivery slots are available for our installation window (separate note from their commercial team is in the project folder). So the conditionality in the appraisal is satisfied.

Actions:
- The **AT-15 / 47-turbine layout is now the project basis.** The earlier AT-12 / 58-turbine layout is superseded — please stop working to it.
- Sophie — the consent envelope (rotor swept area, layout) needs to reflect the AT-15 for the EIA, including the bird collision modelling.
- Priya — cost basis changes; fewer-but-larger machines. The finance model will need updating.

The formal turbine-selection memo will follow and will be the controlled record of this decision.

Ravi
