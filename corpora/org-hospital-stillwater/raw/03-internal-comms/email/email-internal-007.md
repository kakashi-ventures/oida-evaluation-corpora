Team,

Decision on the escalation mechanism, so we can lock v2.0 §6.

Priyanka and Janet's March 20 draft proposed a dedicated sepsis nurse, one per shift on the ED and 4West. I took it to the Staffing Committee. Under the FY2026 position-control freeze (Nurse Staffing Policy HR-NS-002), ~6 net-new FTE is not fundable this cycle, and the CNO/CFO declined to sponsor an in-year exception.

So we're adopting the alternative: **the charge nurse carries the sepsis-alert pager** on each shift. No net-new FTE. It's a weaker form of single-point accountability — the charge nurse carries it alongside everything else — and I want that trade-off named, not buried. If the pilot shows the pager isn't enough, we make the dedicated-role case again in the FY2027 budget.

The adopted memo is finalized (April 1) and folds into Protocol v2.0 §6. The dedicated-nurse draft is now superseded.

Karen
