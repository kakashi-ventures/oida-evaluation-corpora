Marcus, all,

I've attached my formal stewardship position memo. I want it on the record before we finalize.

Short version: **one hour for septic shock, yes. A blanket one-hour broad-spectrum mandate for everyone we "suspect," no.** The mortality evidence Marcus cites is robust for shock and weak-to-absent for sepsis without shock. A universal 1-hour clock, enforced as a metric, reliably drives broad-spectrum overuse, *C. diff*, and antibiotics-before-cultures — real harms, for a benefit that isn't demonstrated in the non-shock group.

"Give the extra dose, de-escalate later" sounds harmless but it isn't free, and at scale it's how stewardship programs lose the resistance fight.

My recommendation is to **stratify**: 1-hour for shock, 3-hour for sepsis without shock with stewardship-guided selection. I'll support v2.0 enthusiastically if it stratifies, and I'll file a formal dissent if it imposes a universal 1-hour mandate. The evidence briefing I circulated Feb 1 lays out the citations.

Elena
