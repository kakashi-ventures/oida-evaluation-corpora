All,

Following up on the field safety alert Veramed sent March 5: as of March 30 they have escalated to a **voluntary recall** of the VX-200 infusion pump. The issue is an over-infusion risk when a specific guardrail setting is bypassed — low probability, but they're pulling them.

What this means for us:
- The VX-200 is our standard smart pump on the ED and 4West. We have ~140 affected units.
- **Interim:** for time-critical sepsis antibiotics, pharmacy and nursing will use the backup pump pool and, where necessary, supervised manual administration, until swaps are complete. I've briefed the charge nurses.
- **Replacement:** Veramed is shipping VX-210 units; full swap expected by early May.

Relevant to our RCA: one Q4 look-back case had an antibiotic infusion delayed by a pump programming problem. So this isn't only a device-safety issue — it intersects directly with our door-to-antibiotic goal. I'll keep the committee updated as the swap proceeds. The field alert is now superseded by the recall notice; treat the recall as the current guidance.

Tom
