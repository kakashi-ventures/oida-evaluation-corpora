# Email: Antibiotic timing — we should commit to 1 hour

**From:** Marcus Tran
**To:** Sepsis Steering Committee
**Sent:** March 2, 2026, 7:05 PM
**Subject:** Antibiotic timing — we should commit to 1 hour

---

Colleagues,

I want to make the case plainly: our antibiotic timing target should be **one hour, door to antibiotic, for every patient we suspect of sepsis.**

The evidence on this is, to me, overwhelming — every hour of delay to effective antibiotics is associated with higher mortality. Our own sentinel case waited nearly four hours and died. The 3-hour target in v1.0 is, functionally, permission to be slow. If we set the bar at one hour for everyone, we will save lives, and the measurement is dead simple: one clock, one number, no carve-outs.

I know stewardship will worry about over-treatment. I respect that. But I'd rather give an extra dose of antibiotics to someone who didn't need it than lose another patient because we were debating whether they met "shock" criteria while the clock ran. Speed first; de-escalate later.

Let's commit to a universal 1-hour target in v2.0.

Marcus
