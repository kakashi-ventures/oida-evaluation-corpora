# Meeting 004: Quality & Patient Safety Committee — Protocol v2.0 Decision

**Date:** April 20, 2026
**Location:** Boardroom A
**Attendees:** Karen Holloway (presenting), Yusuf Adeyemi (CMO, chair), Marcus Tran (ED), Elena Soto (ID), Priyanka Rao (ICU), committee members
**Action:** Vote on approval of Sepsis Response Protocol v2.0

---

[12:05] Adeyemi: This is the decision meeting. Karen presents, we vote. Karen.

[12:07] Holloway: Protocol v2.0 for your approval. Four substantive changes from v1.0: mandatory nurse-driven screening; stratified antibiotic timing — one hour for septic shock, three hours for sepsis without shock; a repeat lactate at three hours when the first is ≥ 2; and a charge-nurse sepsis pager for escalation. The stratification carries ID's endorsement; there is no dissent on the committee.

[12:11] Adeyemi: The escalation — this is the pager, not the dedicated nurse?

[12:12] Holloway: Correct. The dedicated sepsis-nurse proposal was not fundable under the FY26 position-control freeze. Staffing declined the in-year exception. We adopted the charge-nurse pager and named the trade-off in the memo: weaker single-point accountability, revisit in FY27 if the pilot shows it's insufficient.

[12:15] Rao: For the record, I proposed the dedicated nurse and I still think it's better. But the pager is a defensible starting point and I support approving v2.0 with it.

[12:17] Adeyemi: Pilot results — what can we actually say?

[12:18] Holloway: Process measures improved clearly: screening from 41% to ~88%, door-to-antibiotic for shock from 155 minutes to ~74. On **mortality, we say nothing** — the pilot was not powered, the window was six weeks, and the aim statement pre-committed to treating pilot mortality as descriptive only. The interim report says this explicitly. We approve on the RCA findings and the process gains, not on mortality.

[12:21] Soto: And the compliance number?

[12:22] Holloway: Still two methods, still unreconciled. v2.0 states that compliance must be interpreted accordingly until we converge on one method of record. That work continues after go-live.

[12:24] Adeyemi: I appreciate the candor about what we don't know. Motion to approve Protocol v2.0, effective May 1. All in favor?

[12:25] *Unanimous. v2.0 approved.*

[12:26] Adeyemi: Approved. Karen, get the board summary ready for May 5. And fix the death-count slide — it should read two attributable, per the final RCA, not three.
