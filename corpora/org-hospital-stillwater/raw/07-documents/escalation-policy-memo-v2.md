# ED→ICU Sepsis Escalation Policy (ADOPTED)

**Date:** April 1, 2026
**Status:** ADOPTED — supersedes the March 20 draft proposal (Proposal A: Dedicated Sepsis Nurse)
**Authors:** Karen Holloway, MD; Janet Mwangi, RN; Priyanka Rao, MD
**Approved into Protocol v2.0 §6**

---

## Decision

The committee did **not** adopt the dedicated-sepsis-nurse proposal. The Nurse Staffing Policy's position-control freeze for FY2026 made ~6 net-new FTE infeasible, and the Staffing Committee declined to sponsor the request this cycle.

**Adopted instead:** a **sepsis-alert pager carried by the charge nurse** on each shift (ED and 4West). No net-new FTE. The charge nurse:

- Receives the sepsis-alert page on any positive screen with hypotension or lactate ≥ 4 mmol/L.
- Confirms time-zero orders are placed and antibiotic delivery is tracked to target.
- Escalates to the ICU resource nurse if a bed or higher level of care is needed.

## Trade-off acknowledged

Single-point accountability is weaker than under the dedicated-nurse proposal, because the charge nurse carries this alongside existing duties. The committee accepted this trade-off as the only option compatible with the FY2026 staffing budget, and will revisit a dedicated role in the FY2027 budget cycle if pilot data show the pager mechanism is insufficient.

## Reference

This policy operates within, and does not amend, the Nurse Staffing Policy (effective January 2025).
