# Board Quality Committee — Sepsis Pathway 2.0 Summary

**Date:** May 5, 2026
**Prepared for:** Board Quality Committee, Q1 close
**Prepared by:** Yusuf Adeyemi, MD (CMO) and Karen Holloway, MD (VP Quality)

---

## Executive summary

Following a January sentinel event, Stillwater Regional chartered Sepsis Pathway 2.0, a cross-functional redesign of adult sepsis care. The Quality & Patient Safety Committee approved the redesigned protocol (v2.0) on April 20; it takes effect May 1.

## Quality highlights (board slide)

- **Root-cause analysis identified 3 preventable sepsis deaths** and significant gaps in screening and antibiotic timing.
- Redesigned protocol mandates nurse-driven screening, stratified antibiotic timing (1-hour for septic shock), and a repeat-lactate trigger.
- Pilot on the ED and 4West showed improved process compliance.

> Editorial note for the board packet: the "3 deaths" bullet is carried over from the **preliminary** RCA. The **final** RCA (Feb 27) adjudicated this down to **2 attributable deaths** (one Q4 case was reclassified as a non-attributable death under a pre-existing DNR/comfort-care order). The slide above was not updated before the packet froze. The accurate figure is 2; Quality will correct the slide in the next cycle.

## On mortality

Early pilot data are **encouraging but not statistically demonstrated**. The pilot was explicitly not powered to detect a mortality change, and no claim of mortality reduction is being made to the board at this time. We will report risk-adjusted mortality (O:E) at the next quarterly review once sufficient post-go-live volume has accrued.
