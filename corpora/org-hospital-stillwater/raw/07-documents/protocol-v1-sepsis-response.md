# Sepsis Response Protocol v1.0 (Adult)

**Date:** June 1, 2024
**Document type:** Clinical protocol — controlled document SR-CP-014
**Effective:** June 1, 2024
**Owner:** Department of Emergency Medicine
**Status header (as printed):** ACTIVE — supersedes the 2019 sepsis order set

---

## 1. Purpose

Standardize the recognition and initial management of adult patients with suspected sepsis at Stillwater Regional Medical Center.

## 2. Screening

Nurse-driven sepsis screening using the SIRS-based screening tool is **available and encouraged** at triage and on inpatient units. Completion is **not mandatory**; a positive screen prompts physician notification.

## 3. Antibiotic timing

For patients with suspected sepsis or septic shock, broad-spectrum antibiotics should be administered **within 3 hours** of the time of recognition ("time zero"). The 3-hour target applies uniformly to sepsis and septic shock.

## 4. Laboratory

- Serum lactate drawn at time zero.
- Blood cultures drawn before antibiotics where feasible.
- A **repeat lactate is not specified** by this protocol.

## 5. Fluids

Crystalloid 30 mL/kg for hypotension or lactate ≥ 4 mmol/L, within 3 hours.

## 6. Escalation

ED attending to consult Critical Care for refractory hypotension. No standing sepsis-alert paging mechanism is defined; escalation is by direct phone call.

## 7. Review cycle

Scheduled biennial review. (This document was overdue for review at the time the Sepsis Pathway 2.0 initiative was chartered.)
