# Sepsis Response Protocol v2.0 (Adult)

**Date:** April 20, 2026
**Document type:** Clinical protocol — controlled document SR-CP-014 (revision 2)
**Approved:** April 20, 2026 by the Quality & Patient Safety Committee
**Effective:** May 1, 2026
**Owner:** Sepsis Steering Committee (Emergency Medicine, Critical Care, ID/Stewardship, Nursing, Pharmacy)
**Status header (as printed):** ACTIVE — supersedes Sepsis Response Protocol v1.0 (June 2024)

---

## 1. Purpose

Replace Protocol v1.0 with a recognition-first, stratified pathway for adult sepsis and septic shock, incorporating the findings of the January 2026 sentinel-event root-cause analysis.

## 2. Screening — now mandatory

Nurse-driven sepsis screening is **mandatory** at ED triage and at every shift assessment on 4West and the ICU. A positive screen triggers the sepsis-alert pathway (see §6) and is documented as a discrete EHR field.

## 3. Antibiotic timing — stratified

The single 3-hour target of v1.0 is replaced by a stratified target:

- **Septic shock** (hypotension requiring vasopressors or lactate ≥ 4 mmol/L): broad-spectrum antibiotics **within 1 hour** of recognition.
- **Sepsis without shock:** antibiotics **within 3 hours** of recognition, with antimicrobial selection guided by the stewardship-endorsed empiric table (Appendix B).

This stratification was adopted explicitly to focus the 1-hour mandate on the population in which the evidence is strongest (shock), while avoiding a blanket 1-hour broad-spectrum mandate for undifferentiated sepsis. See the Antimicrobial Stewardship position memo (March 2026) for the rationale.

## 4. Laboratory

- Serum lactate at time zero.
- **Repeat lactate at 3 hours** if the initial value is ≥ 2 mmol/L (new in v2.0).
- Blood cultures before antibiotics.

## 5. Fluids

Crystalloid 30 mL/kg for hypotension or lactate ≥ 4 mmol/L, within 3 hours (unchanged from v1.0).

## 6. Escalation

A **sepsis-alert pager carried by the charge nurse** on each unit is activated on any positive screen with hypotension or lactate ≥ 4 mmol/L. (An earlier draft proposed a dedicated sepsis nurse per shift; that proposal was not adopted — see the escalation policy memo.)

## 7. Measurement

SEP-1 3-hour bundle compliance is the primary process measure. The committee notes that, at the time of approval, the two measurement methods (manual abstraction and the automated EHR flag) had **not yet been reconciled**, and compliance must be interpreted accordingly.

## 8. Review cycle

Annual review, or sooner on any sentinel event.
