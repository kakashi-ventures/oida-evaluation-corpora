# DRAFT — ED→ICU Sepsis Escalation Policy (Proposal A: Dedicated Sepsis Nurse)

**Date:** March 20, 2026
**Status:** DRAFT proposal for committee discussion — NOT adopted
**Author:** Priyanka Rao, MD (ICU Director) and Janet Mwangi, RN (Nursing)

---

## Proposal

Establish a **dedicated sepsis nurse, one per shift**, on the ED and on 4West (a new 1.0 FTE per shift, ~3.0 FTE per unit across the rotation). The sepsis nurse would:

- Own the sepsis-alert response end to end.
- Place the time-zero orders, ensure antibiotic delivery within target, and shepherd the repeat-lactate draw.
- Be the single accountable person for bundle completion.

## Rationale

Bundle compliance fails today because no single person owns it; responsibility is diffuse across triage, the primary nurse, and the physician. A dedicated role removes that ambiguity.

## Resource implication

This requires net-new nursing FTE. Per the Nurse Staffing Policy (effective Jan 2025), any change to budgeted unit ratios must go through the Staffing Committee and is subject to the annual position-control budget. At ~6 FTE across two units, this is a material ask in a year with a frozen nursing budget.

## Open question for the committee

Can we fund 6 new FTE? If not, is there a no-new-FTE mechanism that still creates single-point accountability? *(See the adopted v2 escalation memo for the committee's answer.)*
