# Nurse Staffing Policy (HR-NS-002)

**Date:** January 1, 2025
**Document type:** Standing policy — controlled document HR-NS-002
**Effective:** January 1, 2025 (current; reviewed annually, unchanged for 2026)
**Owner:** Chief Nursing Officer / Staffing Committee

---

## 1. Purpose

Define budgeted nurse-to-patient ratios, position control, and the process for any staffing change at Stillwater Regional Medical Center. This is the governing reference for all unit staffing decisions.

## 2. Budgeted ratios (medical-surgical and critical care)

- ICU: 1:2 (1:1 for unstable patients).
- Step-down / 4West: 1:4.
- ED: target 1:4, surge-adjusted.
- Charge nurses are budgeted as a non-assignment resource on each shift where census supports it.

## 3. Position control

Net-new nursing FTE may not be added outside the annual position-control budget. Any in-year request for new FTE must be sponsored by the Staffing Committee and offset within the approved nursing budget. **FY2026 operates under a position-control freeze**: no net-new nursing FTE absent CNO and CFO sign-off.

## 4. Standing nature

This policy is the durable reference for staffing decisions across all quality and operational initiatives. It is not specific to any one project and remains in force unless formally amended by the Staffing Committee.
