# Quality Improvement Charter — Sepsis Pathway 2.0

**Date:** January 26, 2026
**Sponsor:** Yusuf Adeyemi, MD — Chief Medical Officer
**Initiative Lead:** Karen Holloway, MD — VP, Quality & Patient Safety
**Facility:** Stillwater Regional Medical Center (412-bed community teaching hospital, Calhoun County)

---

## Background & Trigger

On January 12, 2026, a sentinel event review was opened following the in-hospital death of an adult patient who presented to the Emergency Department with undifferentiated infection and deteriorated to septic shock. Preliminary review indicated delays in sepsis recognition and antibiotic administration relative to our existing Sepsis Response Protocol (v1.0, effective June 2024).

A retrospective look-back at the prior quarter (Q4 2025) was commissioned as part of the root-cause analysis. Early findings identified **three preventable sepsis-related deaths** associated with bundle-compliance failures. (Note: the death-attribution count is under active review by the RCA team and should be treated as preliminary pending the final report.)

## Aim

Redesign the adult sepsis-response pathway end-to-end ("Sepsis Pathway 2.0") to improve early recognition, shorten time to antibiotics, raise CMS SEP-1 bundle compliance, and reduce risk-adjusted sepsis mortality.

## Scope

- **In scope:** Adult (≥18) patients with suspected sepsis or septic shock presenting to the ED or identified on inpatient units 4West and the ICU.
- **Out of scope:** Pediatric and neonatal sepsis (governed separately by the Pediatrics service line); obstetric sepsis.

## Governance

The Sepsis Steering Committee reports to the Quality & Patient Safety Committee, which holds final approval authority over any protocol change. Board Quality Committee receives a summary at quarter close.

## Target Timeline

RCA final report by end of February; redesigned protocol drafted in March; pilot on 4West and the ED through April; Quality Committee decision in April; phased house-wide rollout thereafter.
