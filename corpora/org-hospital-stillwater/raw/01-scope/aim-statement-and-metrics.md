# Aim Statement & Measurement Plan — Sepsis Pathway 2.0

**Date:** January 28, 2026
**Author:** Karen Holloway, MD (VP Quality) with Sandra Pell (Quality Data Analyst)
**Status:** Approved by Sepsis Steering Committee as the measurement plan of record

---

## Aim Statement

Within two quarters of go-live, Stillwater Regional will improve adult sepsis care such that:

| # | Metric | Baseline (Q4 2025) | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | CMS SEP-1 3-hour bundle compliance | *disputed — see note* | ≥ 90% |
| M2 | Median ED door-to-antibiotic time, suspected septic shock | ~155 min | < 60 min |
| M3 | Nurse-driven sepsis screening completion (ED + 4West) | not measured | ≥ 95% |
| M4 | Risk-adjusted sepsis mortality (O:E ratio) | 1.18 | relative 20% reduction |

## Note on the M1 baseline

The committee could not agree on a single baseline figure for SEP-1 3-hour bundle compliance. Two measurement methods are in use and produce materially different numbers (manual chart abstraction vs. the automated EHR SEP-1 flag). The measurement & analytics workstream is tasked with reconciling the two methods before go-live. **Until reconciled, M1 is reported as a range and no single baseline is endorsed.** This is the most important open measurement question in the initiative.

## Measurement cadence

- Manual SEP-1 abstraction: monthly, on a 30-chart random sample (Quality Dept).
- Automated EHR SEP-1 dashboard: continuous (Clinical Informatics).
- Mortality (O:E): quarterly, risk-adjusted via the existing vendor model; not powered for short-window pilot inference.

## Caveat carried forward

Mortality (M4) is a lagging, low-frequency outcome. The committee agreed in advance that the pilot window will be **too short and too small to support any statistical claim about mortality change**, and that mortality results from the pilot are to be reported as descriptive only, not as evidence of effect.
