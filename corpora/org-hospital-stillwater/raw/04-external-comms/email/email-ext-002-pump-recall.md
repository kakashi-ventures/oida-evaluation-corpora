Dear Mr. Becker,

This notice **supersedes our Field Safety Notice FSN-2026-014 of March 5**. Following completion of our investigation, Veramed is initiating a **voluntary recall** of the VX-200 Infusion Pump.

**Current guidance (this recall notice is now the operative instruction):**
- The earlier "continue use with guardrails enabled" guidance is withdrawn.
- Affected VX-200 units should be transitioned out of service for time-critical infusions as replacement inventory allows.
- Veramed will supply **VX-210** replacement units. For your site (~140 units), shipment is scheduled to complete by **early May 2026**.
- Where immediate replacement is not yet available, use alternative pumps or institutional backup procedures for critical infusions.

We recognize the operational impact and have prioritized your account given your sepsis time-to-antibiotic program. Your Veramed account manager will coordinate the swap schedule.

Veramed Systems, Field Safety Team
