Dear Mr. Becker,

This Field Safety Notice concerns the **Veramed VX-200 Infusion Pump**. We have identified a condition in which, **if the dose-error-reduction guardrail is manually bypassed**, the pump may under-rare circumstances deliver an infusion at a rate higher than programmed.

**Recommended interim action (this notice):**
- Do **not** bypass the guardrail/limit settings on VX-200 pumps.
- Continue normal use with guardrails enabled; risk is associated only with the bypass condition.
- No device return is required at this time.

This is a precautionary notice, not a recall. We are continuing our investigation and will advise if our guidance changes. A corrective action may follow.

Please cascade to nursing and biomed.

Veramed Systems, Field Safety Team
