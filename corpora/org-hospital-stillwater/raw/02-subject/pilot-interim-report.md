# Pilot Interim Report — Sepsis Pathway 2.0 (ED + 4West)

**Date:** April 8, 2026
**Status:** INTERIM / PROVISIONAL — descriptive only; not a final result
**Author:** Sandra Pell (Quality) with Janet Mwangi, RN
**Pilot window:** February 24 – April 6, 2026 (6 weeks), ED and unit 4West

---

## What we piloted

The v2.0 elements ahead of formal approval: mandatory nurse-driven screening, the charge-nurse sepsis pager, stratified antibiotic timing, and the 3-hour repeat-lactate trigger.

## Process measures (encouraging)

- Nurse screening completion (ED): 41% → **~88%**.
- Median door-to-antibiotic, suspected septic shock: 155 min → **~74 min** (target < 60 min; trending toward it).
- Repeat-lactate ordered where indicated: 38% → **~80%**.

These process gains are real and consistent with the design intent.

## Mortality (NOT a result)

During the 6-week pilot there were **4 sepsis deaths among 71 pilot-eligible cases**, versus a comparable prior-period rate that would have predicted ~5–6. **This looks lower, but we cannot and do not claim a mortality effect.** Reasons, stated plainly:

- The sample is tiny (71 cases, 4 events). The confidence interval is enormous.
- Six weeks spans a seasonal decline in respiratory sepsis; the comparison period was mid-winter.
- Case mix was not risk-adjusted in this interim cut.
- The aim statement pre-committed to treating pilot mortality as descriptive only.

**Bottom line: the pilot does not demonstrate, and is not capable of demonstrating, a mortality reduction.** Anyone citing a mortality benefit from this pilot is over-reading the data. A powered O:E read will take several quarters post-go-live.

## Recommendation

Proceed to approval on the strength of the **process** improvements and the RCA findings, not on mortality.
