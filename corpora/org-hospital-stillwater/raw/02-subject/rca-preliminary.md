# Preliminary Root Cause Analysis — Sentinel Event SE-2026-003

**Date:** February 9, 2026
**Status:** PRELIMINARY — not for distribution outside the RCA team; superseded by the final report
**Facilitator:** Karen Holloway, MD (VP Quality)
**Event:** Adult sepsis death, ED, January 12, 2026

---

## Summary

This preliminary analysis is issued at the two-week mark to orient the Sepsis Steering Committee. **Figures here are provisional and will change in the final report.**

## Index event

The index patient presented with fever, tachycardia, and hypotension and was not screened with the nurse-driven tool at triage. Antibiotics were administered approximately 3 hours 40 minutes after arrival. The patient progressed to refractory septic shock and died on hospital day 2.

## Q4 2025 look-back (provisional)

To establish whether the index event was isolated, the team reviewed adult sepsis deaths in Q4 2025. The provisional review identified **three (3) sepsis-related deaths associated with documented bundle-compliance failures** (missed or late antibiotics, no repeat lactate, or no screening). All three are being treated, at this preliminary stage, as potentially preventable.

> ⚠️ Caveat: attribution is not final. At least one of the three cases involved a documented goals-of-care limitation (DNR / comfort-focused care) that may make the death non-attributable to bundle failure. The final report will adjudicate each case.

## Provisional contributing factors

1. Screening not performed / not mandatory under Protocol v1.0.
2. Antibiotic administration beyond the 3-hour target.
3. No repeat-lactate trigger to catch deterioration.

## Next steps

Case-by-case attribution adjudication; interview ED and nursing staff; reconcile the timeline against the EHR audit log. Final report targeted for end of February.
