# Final Root Cause Analysis — Sentinel Event SE-2026-003

**Date:** February 27, 2026
**Status:** FINAL — supersedes the Preliminary RCA of February 9, 2026
**Facilitator:** Karen Holloway, MD (VP Quality)
**Review panel:** Holloway, Tran (ED), Rao (ICU), Soto (ID), Mwangi (Nursing), Becker (Pharmacy)

---

## 1. Scope

Root-cause analysis of the January 12, 2026 adult sepsis death (SE-2026-003), with a Q4 2025 look-back to determine whether the event reflected a systemic pattern.

## 2. Corrected death attribution

The preliminary report cited three Q4 2025 sepsis deaths with bundle-compliance failures. On case-by-case adjudication the panel **reclassified one of the three**: that patient had a documented DNR and a comfort-focused goals-of-care order in place before the sepsis episode, and the death was determined to be **expected and not attributable** to bundle failure.

**Final determination: two (2) attributable, potentially preventable sepsis deaths** in Q4 2025, plus the January index event. The "three deaths" figure from the preliminary report should not be cited; it was superseded by this adjudication.

## 3. Contributing factors (final)

1. **Recognition gap.** Nurse-driven screening was optional under Protocol v1.0 and was not performed in 2 of the 3 index/look-back cases.
2. **Antibiotic delay.** Door-to-antibiotic time exceeded the 3-hour target in the index case (≈3h40m) and in one look-back case.
3. **No deterioration trigger.** Absence of a repeat-lactate requirement delayed recognition of progression to shock.
4. **Infusion delay (one case).** In one look-back case, antibiotic infusion was delayed by an infusion-pump programming issue; this finding prompted the pharmacy review that later intersected with the Veramed VX-200 field alert (see Pharmacy correspondence, March 2026).

## 4. Recommendations

- Mandate nurse-driven screening (adopted in Protocol v2.0).
- Stratified antibiotic timing with a 1-hour target for septic shock (adopted in v2.0; see stewardship memo for the non-shock rationale).
- Add a repeat-lactate trigger at 3 hours (adopted in v2.0).
- Reconcile the two SEP-1 compliance measurement methods before go-live (open).

## 5. Note on measurement

The panel was unable to state a single SEP-1 baseline compliance figure because the manual-abstraction and automated-EHR methods disagree materially. This is flagged as an unresolved measurement issue, not a finding of fact.
